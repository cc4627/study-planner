Soccer

 

Result: the bookmakers' favourite from the average odds, margin taken out. Goals and both to score: from the odds. Corners and yellow cards: models of each side's recent corners and shots, cards and fouls, the referee and the match odds, fitted on 2021–24 and tested on later seasons. Prices: the average bookmaker odds where the data has them (result, goals); elsewhere the fair price, 1 ÷ chance, which a bet has to beat to be worth it. The match double (favourite + goals) and the match acca (every call on the game) multiply the single prices, using the fair price for corners, cards and both to score, so the acca payout is an estimate; bookmakers sell same-game bets as bet builders at their own, usually shorter, price. Domestic fixtures and results from football-data.co.uk (results can take a day or two); Champions, Europa and Conference League from ESPN's free feed, with DraftKings odds.
