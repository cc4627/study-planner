Pick: the player with the higher Elo rating, built from every ATP and WTA singles result since January 2024 on ESPN's free scores feed; the bar splits the match between the two. First set and total games come from a game-by-game model of each match tuned to real game totals. Prices marked @ are bookmaker odds (average over/under for games) from tennisexplorer.com; the € figures use them, and the fair price (1 ÷ chance) where there are none, marked est. First-set prices and the match acca (winner, first set and games together) use the fair price, so their € figures are estimates. "New" marks a player with few rated matches. TBC: the tournament hasn't given the match a slot yet, so it may move to another day. A guide, not a tip; no bets are placed. Help: BeGambleAware · GAMSTOP
